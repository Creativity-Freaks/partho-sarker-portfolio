# Project Summary

Partho Sarker Dhrubo Portfolio is a responsive personal website that showcases bioinformatics research, molecular docking work, agricultural innovation ideas, leadership experience, and field/lab photography.

## Core Goals

- Present academic and research identity clearly
- Highlight selected projects and poster work
- Surface direct contact and social channels
- Keep the experience lightweight, animated, and mobile-friendly

## Main Sections

- Hero
- About
- Research
- Skills
- Leadership
- Lab & Field gallery
- Moments gallery
- CV
- Contact

## Notes

- SEO metadata is configured in route files
- Images are rendered from asset metadata with local fallbacks
- The site uses TanStack Router/Start conventions with a root shell in `src/routes/__root.tsx`
